Questions retailers ask

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone with Android 6.0 or above.

What if the customer switches off the phone or removes the SIM?

The lock is device-owner grade and stays active after reboots. If there is no internet, you can still lock or unlock by SMS.

Can a locked customer still call for help?

Yes. Emergency calls always stay available, and there is a night-time window when the phone is not locked.

What if a customer forgets their PIN after paying?

You can clear the screen password remotely from the retailer app.

Does the customer need to download anything?

No. The customer app installs automatically during QR enrollment.
